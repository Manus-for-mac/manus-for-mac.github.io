Manus is a desktop app that brings professional-grade hand tracking and VR/AR input to creators and developers. It enables natural hand interactions across VR, motion capture, and interactive applications using wrist/hand-worn sensors.

Precise Hand Tracking

Capture detailed finger and hand motion for realistic interaction and animation. High-fidelity tracking of individual finger joints and gestures ensures professional accuracy.

Low-Latency Input

Deliver responsive hand data with minimal delay for immersive experiences and real-time applications. Real-time streaming keeps interactions fluid and precise.

Cross-Platform Integration

Seamless support for VR/AR systems, game engines, and mocap pipelines. SDKs and plugins for Unity, Unreal, and other workflows streamline implementation.

Developer Tools

Easy calibration, tuning, and custom gesture definition. Export motion data or stream directly to applications for animation, analysis, or control.

Reliable Performance

Robust tracking across varied lighting and environments, indoors or outdoors. Multi-user support enables collaborative VR experiences.

How It Works

1. Setup Sensors

Pair your wrist/hand-worn sensors with Manus via Bluetooth. The app handles connection and initial synchronization automatically.

2. Calibrate Tracking

Use built-in tools to calibrate for your hand size and environment. Quick tuning optimizes precision for fingers and gestures.

3. Stream Data

Enable real-time streaming to Unity, Unreal, or other apps. Low-latency input delivers natural hand controls instantly.

4. Integrate and Export

Leverage SDKs for custom integrations or export motion data for animation and analysis. Scale to multi-user setups as needed.

FAQ

What hardware is required?

Manus works with compatible wrist/hand-worn sensors. No additional cameras or base stations needed—tracking relies on sensor data alone.

Which platforms does it support?

Full integration with Unity, Unreal Engine, and major VR/AR systems. Plugins handle common mocap workflows out of the box.

Is calibration difficult?

No—simple guided calibration takes under a minute. Tools adapt to different users, hand sizes, and environments.

Can it track multiple users?

Yes, multi-user mode supports collaborative VR sessions with independent tracking for each participant.
